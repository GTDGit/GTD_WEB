Slide 02 | Company Snapshot

A shared company foundation with two AI product tracks.

GTD is building product infrastructure around two adjacent needs in Indonesia: trusted digital onboarding for regulated businesses, and Indonesian-language transaction execution for everyday reseller commerce.

Parent Company Narrative
GTD acts as the parent operating company and shared technical base behind Vison and Sona.
GTD has three products in its wider portfolio, with Vison and Sona representing the two AI tracks in this deck.
Both products benefit from the same product engineering discipline: API-first design, operational controls, and local market context.
The immediate objective is focused product hardening for real onboarding and transaction workflows, not broad experimentation.
GTD's differentiation is practical domain focus: local onboarding requirements on one side, and Indonesia-native commerce behavior on the other.

Where GTD is focused

Fintechs, digital platforms, and reseller-led transaction channels that require local workflows, controlled automation, and clear operating guardrails.

Why now

Indonesian onboarding expectations are rising while commerce behavior remains chat-first, creating room for applied AI products with local context.

What already exists

Shared APIs, product prototypes, hosted verification flows, and transaction workflow logic are already shaping the GTD stack.

What GTD needs next

More stable model iteration, safer LLM evaluation, and a more durable infrastructure plan for product hardening.

What success looks like

Pilot-ready product infrastructure that is clearer to deploy, easier to monitor, and better aligned with enterprise expectations.
Slide 03 | Market Problem

Three practical gaps GTD is addressing in the Indonesian market.

The opportunity is not abstract. These are workflow-level problems that repeatedly appear in regulated onboarding and distributor-style transaction channels.

KYC

Onboarding trust is harder to maintain

Photo-only checks are increasingly insufficient. Buyers want stronger liveness controls, document validation, and audit-friendly verification artifacts.
API

eKYC workflows are still fragmented

Many teams still combine manual review, point vendors, and custom logic. That makes implementation slower, harder to maintain, and harder to explain during procurement.
NLP

Reseller commerce remains chat-first

Transaction activity already happens inside messaging apps, but safe automation needs better natural-language understanding, confirmation logic, and tool execution discipline.

Why this matters for Vison

Regulated onboarding teams need one clearer path for capture, verification, review, and storage rather than stitching together multiple moving pieces.

Why this matters for Sona

Reseller workflows need language-aware automation that can help complete real tasks without losing confirmation discipline or operational visibility.

Why this matters now

These problems now sit at the intersection of compliance, user trust, and AI-assisted workflow design, making them timely product opportunities rather than future-looking concepts.

Slide 04 | Product 01

Vison is GTD's identity verification workflow for regulated onboarding.

Vison overview

Vison is being designed as a practical eKYC layer for Indonesian businesses that need to capture, validate, compare, and store onboarding evidence in a structured way.

Delivered through API integration, JS SDK surfaces, and a hosted verification flow for different implementation needs.
Designed around real onboarding steps rather than standalone model demos.
Built for fintech onboarding, reseller verification, and other identity-sensitive customer journeys.

Capture and liveness

Selfie capture, passive liveness logic, and challenge-ready flows that help move beyond static photo checks.

Document understanding

ID OCR, field extraction, and document-level validation logic aimed at reducing low-quality manual checks.

Face comparison

Selfie-to-document comparison and identity matching steps intended for structured verification outcomes.

Audit-ready outputs

Verdicts, artifacts, and logging paths that make verification easier to review internally and explain externally.

What Vison needs next

Vison needs repeatable GPU-backed training, benchmark discipline, and pilot-ready inference deployment so the product can move from prototype quality to enterprise-ready execution.

Slide 05 | Product 02

Sona adds an Indonesian-language transaction layer on top of PPOB.ID workflows.

Sona on PPOB.ID

Sona is GTD's conversational layer for transaction assistance, designed to help users complete routine commerce flows using natural Indonesian-language interactions rather than rigid commands.

Designed for conversational transaction requests that feel natural inside chat-based commerce flows.
Maps user requests into existing PPOB.ID workflows instead of treating them as isolated chatbot prompts.
Keeps confirmation and execution grounded in business rules rather than unconstrained assistant behavior.

Language-aware intent handling

Designed around Indonesian-language transaction phrasing, short forms, and practical reseller communication patterns.

Tool calling over GTD workflows

Requests can be routed into the existing operational stack rather than treated as isolated conversational output.

Confirmation discipline

Transaction assistance is paired with explicit confirmation and session control instead of uncontrolled autonomous execution.

Catalog coverage

The product direction includes common bill, balance, top-up, and reseller catalog actions already familiar to PPOB workflows.

What Sona needs next

Sona needs disciplined model evaluation, guarded orchestration, and clearer conversation controls so natural-language transaction assistance can stay useful without sacrificing safety.
