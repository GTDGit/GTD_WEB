The Problem

Three Unsolved Problems
Holding Indonesia's Fintech Back

01

KYC Fraud & Deepfake Attacks

Synthetic face generation and deepfake video technology now bypass conventional photo-based KYC with over 90% success rate. Indonesian OJK-regulated fintechs face mounting compliance risk with no standardized AI-based defense layer available as a managed service.

⚠ 340% increase in AI-generated identity fraud cases in SEA, 2023–2025
02

No Unified eKYC Infrastructure

Every fintech startup in Indonesia rebuilds identity verification from scratch — spending 12–18 months and $200K+ on systems that are non-compliant, poorly maintained, and unable to meet NIST SP 800-63B or iBeta PAD requirements demanded by enterprise procurement.

⚡ Average time-to-compliance: 14 months without a PaaS solution
03

UMKM Excluded from Automation

Indonesia's 64 million micro and small businesses transact via WhatsApp manually — no AI-native commerce layer exists that understands Bahasa Indonesia, integrates with payment rails, and operates within the everyday flow of resellers' work.

🎯 Addressable: 10M+ UMKM resellers in digital product distribution

Regulatory & Standards Compliance

Built to Meet Every Standard
That Matters

🏛️
INTERNATIONAL

NIST SP 800-63B

Digital Identity Guidelines for identity proofing and authentication assurance levels. The primary international benchmark referenced by global financial institutions and enterprise fintech procurement teams evaluating biometric KYC vendors.

🧪
CERTIFICATION

iBeta PAD Level 1 & 2

Presentation Attack Detection certification per ISO/IEC 30107-3. iBeta Level 2 is the highest independent liveness certification available, required by Tier-1 fintech enterprise procurement in Southeast Asia and demanded by international banking partners.

🇮🇩
INDONESIA — OJK

POJK eKYC Regulation

OJK's electronic Know Your Customer regulation governing biometric-based identity verification for all licensed financial service providers in Indonesia — the primary domestic compliance requirement for Vison's B2B client base.

🔒
INDONESIA — DATA

UU PDP No. 27/2022

Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law — biometric data, selfies, and OCR outputs are stored exclusively within AWS ap-southeast-3 Jakarta region, ensuring full territorial data sovereignty compliance for enterprise clients.
