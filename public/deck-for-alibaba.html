The Problem

Identity Fraud & Financial Exclusion
Cost Indonesia Billions Annually

Legacy KYC processes are slow, expensive, and easily bypassed. Meanwhile, millions of UMKM micro-businesses lack access to seamless digital financial tools.

01

KYC Fraud on the Rise

Deepfake technology and synthetic identity attacks bypass traditional photo-based verification, exposing fintechs and banks to massive compliance risk under POJK eKYC regulation.

02

Fragmented Identity Infrastructure

No unified B2B eKYC PaaS exists for Indonesian SME fintechs. Each company builds their own — expensive, non-compliant, and difficult to scale across Southeast Asia.

03

UMKM Left Behind

10 million+ Indonesian micro-resellers transact via WhatsApp manually. No AI-powered transaction automation exists that understands Bahasa Indonesia naturally and integrates with payment rails.

Compliance & Standards

Built to Meet the Strictest
Global & Indonesian Standards

Vison is designed from the ground up to satisfy regulators, enterprise procurement, and international certification bodies.

🏛️

NIST SP 800-63B

Digital Identity Guidelines for identity proofing and authentication — the international benchmark for biometric systems used by global financial institutions.

🧪

iBeta PAD Level 1 & 2

Presentation Attack Detection certification targeting ISO/IEC 30107-3 compliance — required for enterprise fintech KYC procurement in Southeast Asia.

🇮🇩

POJK eKYC

OJK's electronic Know Your Customer regulation governing identity verification for Indonesian licensed financial institutions and digital lenders.

🔒

UU PDP No. 27/2022

Indonesian Personal Data Protection Law — biometric and KYC data stored exclusively in AWS ap-southeast-3 Jakarta, never leaving Indonesian territory.

📋

Perpres 83/2021

Presidential Regulation on Identity Data — NIK validation and population data access governance for digital service providers in Indonesia.

🌐

PSE / TDPSE Registered

Registered Electronic System Provider with Kominfo — mandatory for all digital platforms operating in Indonesia under Government Regulation 71/2019.
